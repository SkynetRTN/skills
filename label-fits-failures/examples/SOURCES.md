# Reference image sources

Every image here was extracted unchanged (some cropped to one panel) from
the Skynet Robotic Telescope Network's UNC Astro 101 lab units (WebAssign,
2020; author Dan Reichart). Page numbers are PDF pages of the printed
assignment previews.

| File | Unit, page | Caption in the unit |
|---|---|---|
| `clouds_total_a.png`, `clouds_total_b.png` | Lab 1, p. 8 (both panels of one figure) | "clouds blocking all of the light ... all that remains to be seen is the response pattern of the CCD and out-of-focus dust on the camera window" |
| `clouds_partial.png` | Lab 1, p. 8 (left panel) | "clouds blocking some of the light (left vs. right)" |
| `nominal_same_field_clear.png` | Lab 1, p. 8 (right panel of the same figure) | the clear comparison frame |
| `wind_or_mount_shake.png` | Lab 1, p. 8 | "wind or mount misbehavior distorting or streaking the images" |

| `not_tracking.png` | Lab 1, p. 9 | "the mount not tracking Earth's rotation" |
| `out_of_focus.png` | Lab 1, p. 9 | "focus misbehavior" |
